# specs.md: Eigenspace Stress Decomposition & CMBR Invariant Trading Engine

## 1. System Vision & Objective
Extract the invariant "Cosmic Microwave Background" (CMBR) of the S&P 500 cross-asset manifold. Instead of predicting noisy directional price ticks, the engine:
1. Constructs rolling cross-sectional return and sensitivity manifolds across S&P 500 constituents.
2. Filters out idiosyncratic microstructure noise (TV static) using **Random Matrix Theory (Marchenko-Pastur Law)**.
3. Isolates the persistent, non-zero dominant **eigenvectors** (the structural stress dials).
4. Generates trading rules based on **orthogonal residual dislocations**: when a stock deviates significantly from the subspace spanned by the dominant eigenvectors, execute a mean-reverting or momentum-arbitrage rebalance back toward the manifold.

## 3. Data Pipeline & Alignment

### 3.1 Input Sources (from Kaggle Dataset)
* **`prices.csv` / Ticker CSVs:** Daily Adjusted Close prices across $N \approx 500$ stocks over 15 years.
* **`metadata.csv` / `01_company_info.csv`:** Sector / Industry classifications (used for sector-neutral validation).
* **`financials.csv` & `news_sentiment.csv`:** (Optional secondary filters to veto fundamentally broken stocks).

### 3.2 Matrix Construction
1. **Return Matrix ($R \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times N}$):**
   $$r_{i,t} = \ln(P_{i,t} / P_{i,t-1})$$
   Standardize returns across a rolling window $T$ (e.g., $T = 252$ trading days):
   $$z_{i,t} = \frac{r_{i,t} - \mu_i}{\sigma_i}$$
2. **Empirical Correlation Matrix ($C \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times N}$):**
   $$C = \frac{1}{T} Z^T Z$$

## 4. The CMBR Extraction Algorithm (Noise Filtering)

### 4.1 Marchenko–Pastur Separation
Under the null hypothesis that $C$ is generated by pure random noise, its eigenvalues $\lambda$ are bounded by:
$$\lambda_{\max} = \sigma^2 \left(1 + \sqrt{\frac{N}{T}}\right)^2, \quad \lambda_{\min} = \sigma^2 \left(1 - \sqrt{\frac{N}{T}}\right)^2$$
*(where $\sigma^2 = 1 - \sum \lambda_k / N$ for non-noise components)*.

* **Noise Bulk ($\lambda \le \lambda_{\max}$):** High-frequency TV static. Set to a constant trace-preserving floor or zeroed out.
* **Signal Eigenspace ($\lambda > \lambda_{\max}$):** The **CMBR Modes** $V_{\text{signal}} = [v_1, v_2, \dots, v_k]$ (typically 3 to 7 dominant eigenvectors).
  - $v_1$: Market Mode (aggregate systemic liquidity / beta).
  - $v_2, v_3$: Sector / Duration / Factor Rotation dials.

## 5. Mathematical Trading Rule: Manifold Projection & Residual Arbitrage

### 5.1 Orthogonal Projection
Project the daily market return vector $\mathbf{r}_t \in \mathbb{R}^N$ onto the clean structural subspace:
$$\hat{\mathbf{r}}_t = V_{\text{signal}} V_{\text{signal}}^T \mathbf{r}_t$$

### 5.2 The Residual / Dislocation Vector ($\mathbf{e}_t$)
$$\mathbf{e}_t = \mathbf{r}_t - \hat{\mathbf{r}}_t$$
* $\hat{\mathbf{r}}_t$ is what the collective structural web demanded the stocks do.
* $\mathbf{e}_t$ is the idiosyncratic deviation.

### 5.3 Execution Trigger
Compute the rolling cumulative Z-score of the residual for each asset:
$$s_{i,t} = \frac{\text{CumResidual}_{i,t} - \mu_{e,i}}{\sigma_{e,i}}$$

* **Long Signal:** $s_{i,t} < -2.0$ (Stock lagged behind its structural eigenvector projection; elastic rubber band stretched downward).
* **Short Signal:** $s_{i,t} > +2.0$ (Stock surged ahead of its structural eigenvector projection; elastic rubber band stretched upward).
* **Exit / Flat:** When $s_{i,t}$ crosses back through $0.0$.
* **Stop-Loss / Regime Break:** If $\vert{}s_{i,t}\vert{} > 4.0$, close immediately (indicates an idiosyncratic structural break, e.g., fraud or acquisition, where the link snapped).

## 6. Backtesting Rig & Execution Parameters

* **Universe:** S&P 500 constituents with $>95\%$ continuous trading history over the 15-year period.
* **Rolling Window:** $T = 126$ to $252$ trading days for eigenvector estimation.
* **Re-estimation Frequency:** Re-calculate $V_{\text{signal}}$ every 5 trading days (avoids high-turnover churn while allowing dials to adapt).
* **Position Sizing:** Dollar-neutral basket allocation. Arbitrary scaling bounded by maximum portfolio leverage (e.g., max gross exposure 1.0x).
* **Slippage & Commission Model:** 5 basis points per transaction side.
* **Target Metrics:**
  - Annualized Sharpe Ratio ($> 1.5$ target).
  - Information Coefficient (IC) of residual mean-reversion.
  - Maximum Drawdown ($< 15\%$).
